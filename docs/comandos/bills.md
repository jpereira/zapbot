# `/bills`

Groups only: draws 2 different members (not counting the bot) and tags them to "pay a boleto".

```text
/bills
🥳 Congratulations @Fulano and @Beltrano 🎉
You were picked to pay a bill! 💸✨
Write down the number: 📝 001 9 337370000000100 05009 401448 16060680935031
Good luck paying it! 😉💰
```

Groups with fewer than 2 members (not counting the bot) are refused with
`Not enough members in the group.`, and outside a group the bot warns that `/bills` only works in
groups.
