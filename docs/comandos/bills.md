# `/bills` (`/boletos`)

Groups only: draws 2 different members (not counting the bot) and tags them to "pay a boleto".

```text
/bills
🥳 Parabéns @Fulano e @Beltrano 🎉
Vocês foram sorteados para pagar um boleto! 💸✨
Anote o número: 📝 001 9 337370000000100 05009 401448 16060680935031
Boa sorte pagando! 😉💰
```

Groups with fewer than 2 members (not counting the bot) are refused with
`Membros insuficientes no grupo.`, and outside a group the bot warns that `/bills` only works in
groups.
